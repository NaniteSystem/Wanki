# П3 «Холст» — Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Make writing on pages fast and complete:
- a page with 3000 strokes stays smooth;
- the eraser can erase part of a stroke and has three sizes;
- the lasso can copy, paste, duplicate, resize, rotate, recolour and move to another page;
- text is recognised line by line, a line's text can be corrected, and a found line is highlighted;
- pages hold text blocks and pictures;
- new paper types and «Бесконечная» pages;
- a held pen straightens the stroke into a line;
- pages export to PDF and PDFs or pictures can be imported as page backgrounds.

**Architecture:**
- `core/ink` gets the pure pieces: `InkCodec` v3 (one self-contained chunk per stroke, so unchanged strokes are never re-encoded), a uniform `SpatialGrid`, affine transforms, the stroke eraser cut and the straight-line rule. All are JVM-tested.
- `InkLayer` keeps the grid, the per-stroke chunks and the page objects in step with every edit. Pages (and boards) draw finished ink from bitmap tiles rendered off the main thread. Only dirty tiles are redrawn.
- **DB v10** adds `page_objects` (text blocks and pictures), `page_lines` (recognised and corrected text per line) and a page background (a picture, or one page of an imported PDF kept as its original file).
- PDF export draws strokes as filled outline paths on `android.graphics.pdf.PdfDocument` and shares the file through a `FileProvider`. Import renders backgrounds on demand through `PdfRenderer`.

**Tech Stack:** Kotlin 2.3, Compose, Room, androidx.ink 1.1.0-alpha07 (rendering, stroke meshes), ML Kit Digital Ink, `android.graphics.pdf.PdfDocument` / `PdfRenderer`, `androidx.core.content.FileProvider`, Activity Result contracts `PickVisualMedia`, `TakePicture`, `OpenMultipleDocuments`. No new third-party libraries.

## Decisions (made here; the user delegates them)

1. **InkCodec v3 = one length-prefixed, self-contained chunk per stroke** (positions and time restart per stroke). Saving joins cached chunks. *If wrong:* another format version later; v1–v3 stay readable, nothing is lost.
2. **Spatial index = uniform grid, 256 world units per cell** (`SpatialGrid` in `core/ink`). *If wrong:* very long strokes on boards sit in many cells (slower queries); swap in an R-tree behind the same interface.
3. **Tiles**:
   - 512 × 512 px bitmaps at power-of-two zoom levels, ink only (paper, backgrounds and objects are drawn separately);
   - rendered on one background thread; LRU budget of 64 MB, or `memoryClass / 4` MB when that is smaller;
   - a dirty tile is drawn stroke by stroke until its bitmap is ready;
   - only canvases with a viewport (pages, boards) use tiles; note blocks and review cells keep direct drawing.

   *If wrong:* memory pressure on low-end phones, or a frame of direct drawing after big edits. The direct path stays as the fallback.
4. **«Бесконечная» page size is in П3** (the tiles make it cheap):
   - portrait only, width 1000;
   - the sheet grows in steps of 707 units (half a page) so there is always at least half a page below the lowest ink;
   - PDF export cuts it into A4-high sheets.

   *If wrong:* the paper task grows by a day; it can be cut back to A4/A5 without touching data.
5. **Eraser**:
   - default mode is «Частично», size «Средний»;
   - the sizes are radius **8 / 16 / 32 dp** on screen, so the eraser is the same size at any zoom;
   - it is remembered per section, apart from the pen (`Settings.eraser(section)`);
   - the pen's eraser end uses the same settings.

   *If wrong:* people used to whole-stroke erasing are surprised once; it is one tap to switch.
6. **Partial eraser cut**:
   - the eraser covers a capsule between consecutive samples, so fast drags leave no gaps;
   - a stroke is cut where its segments cross the capsule edge, and pressure, tilt and time are interpolated at the cut;
   - pieces shorter than `max(brush size, 2)` world units are dropped.

   *If wrong:* tiny leftovers or slightly short cuts; only the cut function changes.
7. **Lasso transform**:
   - always about the selection centre: one corner handle scales uniformly (0.1×–10×), one handle above the frame rotates;
   - brush widths and text size scale with it;
   - there is no stretching to a different aspect.

   *If wrong:* stretching has to be added later as another handle.
8. **Ink clipboard** is in memory and app-wide. It survives switching pages and notebooks, but not process death. «Вставить» puts the clip at the centre of the view. *If wrong:* a copy is lost when the process dies; nothing on a page is lost.
9. **«На другую страницу»**:
   - only in notebooks (not on diary days or boards);
   - written in one repository transaction (target first, then source);
   - the snackbar «Перенесено на стр. N» · «Отменить» reverses both pages;
   - canvas undo on the source brings the strokes back as a copy, so they can be duplicated but never lost;
   - strokes are shifted inside the target page if they would fall off it.

   *If wrong:* an occasional duplicate after a canvas undo.
10. **Page objects** (`page_objects`):
    - kinds `TEXT` and `IMAGE` now; `MASK` is reserved for П4;
    - drawn under the ink, so you can write on a picture;
    - inserted from the header menu («Текстовый блок», «Картинка»), because the toolbar is full;
    - not on boards (П7 replaces boards with items).

    *If wrong:* the insert actions are less visible; they move when П4 reworks the toolbar.
11. **Pictures**:
    - stored in the media folder as `page-<uuid>.jpg` (or `.png` when the image has alpha), downscaled to ≤ 2048 px on the long side, JPEG quality 90;
    - unreferenced `page-*` media are deleted by the daily trash purge;
    - a page still in the trash keeps its media.

    *If wrong:* some loss of detail on very large photos.
12. **PDF import keeps the original PDF** (`page-<uuid>.pdf`):
    - each page refers to it by page index and renders on demand through `PdfRenderer` (one at a time, LRU of 3 bitmaps);
    - pictures become backgrounds as files;
    - a page is landscape when the source page is wider than tall, and the background is fitted and centred;
    - imports are capped at 300 pages.

    *If wrong:* a slower first render of a background, and a large PDF lives in the media folder.
13. **PDF export**:
    - strokes are filled outline paths from the androidx.ink mesh (vector); paper lines and text blocks are vector too; pictures and backgrounds are raster;
    - sizes: A4 = 595 × 842 pt, A5 = 420 × 595 pt (swapped for landscape);
    - files go to `cache/exports/` and are shared through `FileProvider` authority `${applicationId}.files`; exports older than 1 day are cleared at start.

    *If wrong:* highlighter strokes overlap darker than on screen; only the PDF drawer changes.
14. **Recognition by line**:
    - `page_lines` are keyed by `(pageId, lineKey)`; `lineKey` is an order-independent hash of the line's stroke chunks;
    - only new keys are sent to ML Kit;
    - a failed line keeps nothing and is retried next time, while the other lines are still stored;
    - a correction stays while its key exists;
    - `page_ink.recognizedText` stays the joined text (corrected over recognised), so the current search keeps working.

    *If wrong:* when `LineSegmenter` regroups neighbours, their lines are re-recognised and their corrections dropped.
15. **The found line is highlighted in П3**:
    - this uses `page_lines` and the existing notebook search (П4's general search is not needed);
    - `Route.Notebook` gets `highlight`;
    - matching lines get a yellow box for 4 s, and the view scrolls to the first one.

    *If wrong:* a small amount of work that П4 may reroute.
16. **Straight line**:
    - after ≥ 24 dp of drawing, holding the pen within 6 dp for **500 ms** turns the stroke into a line from its start to the pen;
    - the line then follows the pen until it lifts;
    - this works for finger strokes too.

    *If wrong:* an accidental line when someone pauses mid-stroke; tune the thresholds.
17. **The page view is saved 1 s after a pan or zoom ends**, not only on exit (closes the П2 follow-up). *If wrong:* a few extra small writes.
18. **An unreadable ink blob** opens the page read-only with «Страницу не удалось прочитать», and nothing is saved over it. *If wrong:* that page can't be edited until the blob is repaired, but the blob is never lost.

## Review Focus

1. **A page whose stored ink cannot be decoded.** The editor must not save an empty or new drawing over it. Task 2: `PageEditorStateTest.unreadableInkIsNeverOverwritten`.
2. **Process death or a failure in the middle of «На другую страницу».** The strokes end up on at least one page and are never lost. Task 7: `RepositoryTest.moveToPageIsAtomic` (a failing target write leaves the source unchanged).
3. **Degenerate strokes under the partial eraser**: a one-point dot, a stroke of identical points, NaN channels, an eraser larger than the stroke, and a capsule of zero length (a tap). No crash, no zero-point strokes, and channels are kept. Task 5: `StrokeEraserTest.degenerateInputs`.
4. **Imports that are huge, encrypted, empty or broken**: an 8000 × 6000 photo, a password-protected PDF, a 0-page PDF, a truncated file. These give a message and add no pages, or add only whole pages. Task 11: `PageImportTest.badInputsAddNothing`.
5. **Media of a page in the trash.** Pictures and PDF backgrounds survive the daily cleanup until the page is purged, and they come back with «Восстановить». Task 3: `RepositoryTest.mediaGcKeepsTrashedReferences`.
